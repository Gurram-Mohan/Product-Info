📚 Data Sources & Limitations:
Nutritional data is sourced from the USDA FoodData Central (usda.gov) and the ICMR-NIN Indian Food Composition Tables 2017. Daily reference values are based on FSSAI Nutrition Labelling Regulations 2020 and Codex Alimentarius reference values, cross-referenced with ICMR-NIN RDA 2020 for micronutrients. Values are representative estimates for generic food types and may differ from specific brand products. "Data unavailable" indicates that a reliable published value was not found in these sources for that nutrient; it is not equivalent to zero.
